TechCo HR Handbook - Synthetic Practice Document
Version: Demo 1
This is invented training data for the project. It contains no real employee records.

1. Working hours
The working week is Monday to Friday. Office hours are 09:00 to 18:00, including a one-hour lunch break.

2. Annual leave
Each employee has 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year.

3. Casual leave
Each employee has 7 working days of casual leave per calendar year.

4. Sick leave
Each employee has 10 working days of sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required after more than 3 consecutive working days of sick leave.

5. Planned leave requests
Employees must send a planned leave request to their HR manager at least 5 working days in advance. HR records the written approval in the team's leave register.

6. Emergency leave
For an emergency, the employee must notify the HR manager on the same working day. HR records the final decision in the leave register.

7. Remote work
An employee may work remotely for up to 2 days per week with prior written approval from the line manager.

8. Probation
The probation period is 90 calendar days. HR issues written confirmation after the review.

9. Reimbursements
The monthly reimbursement limit is PKR 15,000 per employee. Submit receipts within 7 calendar days of the expense. Line-manager approval is required.

10. HR contact
The fictional training contact is hr@example.com. This example address is not a real company support inbox.

This handbook is a learning fixture, not a real company policy or employment advice.
